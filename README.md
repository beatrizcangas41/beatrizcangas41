## About

**Senior Solutions Manager at Bluebeam · Salesforce & business systems leader · Technical product ownership**

I'm Beatriz, a Senior Solutions Manager at Bluebeam with 8+ years of experience across enterprise platforms, CRM strategy, and cross-functional delivery. My work connects business needs with platform strategy, roadmaps, governance, integrations, and adoption.

My background includes Salesforce administration, GTM and revenue systems, product ownership, automation, data governance, reporting, and user adoption. I've led platform initiatives supporting 1,500+ users and worked across Sales, Marketing, Finance, and Operations, including vendor management and Agile delivery.

I hold degrees in Computer Science and Computer Engineering from Florida Atlantic University. My [Trailblazer profile](https://www.salesforce.com/trailblazer/bcangas97) lists five Salesforce certifications, and my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/beatriz-cangas-perez/) documents my career and Scrum credentials.

Alongside technology, I play right wing for the USA Team Handball Women's National Team and run [Bea Destinations](https://beadestinations.com), where original photography meets practical trip planning.
